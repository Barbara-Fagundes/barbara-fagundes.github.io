Overview

I didn't start out in tech. I started in a classroom in São Paulo, teaching kindergarteners how to read.

That path — early childhood education, then a leap into computer science, then a PhD in engineering education — shapes everything I do. I think about learning from the inside out: what it actually feels like to not understand something, and what it takes to design an experience that helps someone get there.

I recently completed my postdoctoral appointment at Purdue University's Center for Instructional Excellence, where I led research on generative AI adoption in undergraduate CS and engineering courses and contributed to faculty development initiatives grounded in learning science. I am now actively looking for industry roles where research meets practice.

Over the past decade I have studied learning across K-2 classrooms, K-12 STEM settings, and undergraduate courses, and translated those findings into curriculum, instructional materials, and learning experiences that actually work. I bring research rigor, practitioner instincts, and a technical foundation that is not common in this space. I am drawn to teams that care deeply about whether learning actually happens.

What I bring

  • AI in Education and Learning I led qualitative research on generative AI adoption in undergraduate CS and engineering courses: what helps, what backfires, and what instructors can actually do about it. If your team is building or deploying AI-powered learning tools, I have probably already studied the problems you are running into.
  • K-12 STEM Education and Curriculum I designed and studied computational thinking curriculum in K-2 settings as part of NSF-funded research. I know how young learners develop algorithmic reasoning, sequencing, and debugging skills, and what it takes to build teacher-facing materials and assessments that hold up in real classrooms.
  • Learning Science and Instructional Design My PhD-level expertise in how people learn is not just theoretical. I have designed curriculum, assessments, and learning progressions from K-2 to graduate level, grounded in cognitive load theory, self-determination theory, and constructivist learning frameworks.
  • Qualitative and Mixed-Methods Research I am experienced in focus groups, interviews, thematic analysis, classroom video analysis, and survey design. I know how to translate messy, complex research findings into recommendations that product and instructional teams can actually use.
  • Curriculum Development and eLearning Production I have end-to-end experience: content scoping, instructional writing, storyboarding, video production, and LMS deployment. Tools: Articulate 360, Camtasia, Adobe Premiere Pro, Brightspace, Qualtrics, NVivo.
  • Cross-Functional Collaboration I have spent years working across interdisciplinary teams including faculty, engineers, curriculum developers, and project partners on NSF-funded initiatives. I am comfortable at the intersection of research, design, and delivery.
