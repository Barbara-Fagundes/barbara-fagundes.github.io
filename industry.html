Overview

Bárbara Fagundes is a postdoctoral researcher and learning scientist whose industry work is a direct extension of her research agenda: advancing responsible AI integration in learning environments, strengthening computing education, and translating evidence-based findings into instructional practice that scales.

She brings a rare combination to applied work: the analytical rigor of a researcher, the practitioner instincts of a former teacher, and the technical foundation of a computer scientist with hands-on experience in AI-integrated learning environments.

What I bring to industry collaborations

  • Learning science that connects to practice Not just theory. Years of designing and studying real learning environments, from K-2 classrooms to undergraduate STEM courses.
  • AI in education research Currently leading qualitative research on how undergraduate students adopt and ethically use generative AI in CS and engineering courses. Directly relevant to organizations building or deploying AI-powered learning tools.
  • Curriculum and instructional design From K-2 computational thinking curricula to graduate-level online courses. Experience with Articulate 360, Camtasia, Adobe Premiere Pro, Qualtrics, Brightspace, and more.
  • Cross-functional collaboration Comfortable at the intersection of research, design, and delivery. Experienced working with faculty, engineers, curriculum developers, and project partners across NSF-funded initiatives.

Selected Projects

  • Generative AI in Undergraduate CS and Engineering Courses Led a large-scale qualitative study at Purdue examining how students engage with generative AI in technical coursework. Designed protocols, facilitated focus groups, conducted thematic analysis, and translated findings into evidence-based instructional guidance. Contributed to a published arXiv preprint.
  • K-2 Computational Thinking Curriculum — NSF ReCT Project Designed curriculum integrating computational thinking into K-2 literacy. Developed lesson sequences, assessment instruments, and video tutorials for pre-service teachers. Co-authored peer-reviewed publications disseminating findings to the computing education community.
  • E-Learning Modules for Middle School Teachers — CISTAR NSF Designed and produced e-learning modules and video-based instructional supports for middle school teachers. Used Camtasia, Articulate 360, and Adobe Premiere Pro to develop scalable, teacher-facing content.
  • Teaching Engineering Online — Graduate Course Co-developed a graduate course on online engineering pedagogy through an iterative, collaborative process with the faculty member. Contributed to content development, scripting, module structuring, slide production, and Brightspace course management for an asynchronous flipped classroom experience.
