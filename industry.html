Overview

Bárbara Fagundes is a learning scientist and researcher with a rare combination of skills: empirical research expertise, hands-on instructional design experience, and deep knowledge of how people learn in AI-integrated and computing environments.

She recently completed her postdoctoral appointment at Purdue University's Center for Instructional Excellence, where she led research on generative AI adoption in undergraduate CS and engineering courses and contributed to faculty development initiatives grounded in learning science. She is now actively seeking industry roles where research meets practice.

Her work spans over a decade of studying learning — from K-2 classrooms to undergraduate STEM courses — and translating findings into instructional products that work. That combination of research rigor, practitioner instincts, and technical foundation makes her an unusual fit for teams building serious learning experiences.
